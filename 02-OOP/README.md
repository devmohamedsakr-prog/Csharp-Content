# Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) in C#

Object-Oriented Programming is a fundamental paradigm for building scalable, maintainable applications. This section covers the core concepts, four pillars, real-world applications, and advanced patterns.

## 📚 Structure

### 1. **Terms & Concepts** (`01-Terms-and-Concepts`)
Learn fundamental OOP terminology and concepts that form the foundation of object-oriented design.

### 2. **Four Pillars** (`02-Four-Pillars`)
Master the four pillars of OOP: Encapsulation, Inheritance, Polymorphism, and Abstraction.

### 3. **Benefits & Use Cases** (`03-Benefits-and-Use-Cases`)
Understand why OOP matters and explore real-world scenarios where OOP excels.

### 4. **Limitations & Best Practices** (`04-Limitations-and-Best-Practices`)
Recognize OOP limitations and learn best practices to avoid common pitfalls.

### 5. **Examples & Projects** (`05-Examples-and-Projects`)
Practical implementations and complete projects demonstrating OOP principles.

### 6. **OOP Paradigms** (`06-OOP-Paradigms`)
Explore different OOP paradigms: class-based, composition, interfaces, and architectural patterns.

## 🚀 Learning Path

1. Start with **Terms and Concepts** to build vocabulary
2. Deep dive into **Four Pillars** - the heart of OOP
3. Explore **Benefits** to understand when to use OOP
4. Study **Limitations** to know when NOT to use OOP
5. Practice with **Examples and Projects**
6. Master **Paradigms** for advanced architecture
